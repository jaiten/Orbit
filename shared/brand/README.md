# Orbit

**A little space for what matters.**

The mark is an open O with a small companion dot: a daily rhythm with room to
move. Use the coral tile for app icons and the single-color ring/dot on quiet
surfaces. Keep at least one dot-width of clear space around the mark. Never
stretch it, add a drop shadow to the symbol, or use it as a busy background.

Coral `#C43B33`, warm paper `#FFFEFD`, ink `#292723`. Dark mode uses coral
`#FF8C7F` on `#191918`. Use system typography (SF on iOS, Segoe UI on Windows),
generous spacing, restrained separators, and short, practical copy.

The tone is calm and direct. Say “Your plan,” “Open space,” and “Saved locally.”
Avoid guilt, productivity scores, and punishing missed days. Keep the day’s
commitments separate from the inbox; show estimated minutes without imposing a cap.

SVG assets are editable masters. App icons are exported from the same mark.
