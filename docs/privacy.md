# Orbit privacy notice

Orbit is a local-first planner. Tasks, habits, lists and check-ins are stored on
your device. If you create an Orbit account, that planner data is also sent to
Orbit's Supabase project so it can sync between your signed-in devices.

## Data Orbit processes

- Your email address and authentication/session records when you create an account.
- Planner content you choose to sync, including task and habit titles, notes,
  schedules, reminders, tags, subtasks and completion history.
- A device label and last-active time for the active-sign-ins screen.
- Operational service logs maintained by Supabase. Orbit's application diagnostics
  must not include planner titles, passwords, email links or session tokens.

Calendar events, speech audio and notification contents are handled on the device.
Orbit does not upload your Apple Calendar or speech audio to its sync database.

## Why it is processed

Orbit uses this data only to provide authentication, synchronize your planner,
resolve conflicting offline edits, deliver requested reminders and protect account
access. Orbit does not sell personal data or use planner content for advertising.

## Service providers and retention

Supabase hosts authentication and synchronized data in the project's configured
region. Apple and Microsoft operate their respective app platforms and device
services. Synced records are retained while the account exists, including deletion
tombstones needed by offline devices.

Use **Settings → Account → Delete Account** to permanently delete the account and
its synchronized data. This also clears the planner from the device performing the
deletion and blocks other signed-in devices from syncing. Data previously exported
by you, or data still stored locally on another offline device, is outside the
server's control and must be deleted on that device separately.

You can export a portable JSON copy from Settings before deleting your account.

## Contact

For privacy or support requests, use the support contact published with Orbit's App
Store or Windows download listing. The public version of this notice must include
that actual support address and its effective date before beta invitations are sent.
